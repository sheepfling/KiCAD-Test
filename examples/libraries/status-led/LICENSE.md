# Licensing record — status-LED training library

## Template status

The original synthetic symbols and footprints in this directory are published under
[Zero-Clause BSD (0BSD)](https://opensource.org/license/0bsd), as part of the upstream
KiCad workflow scaffold. This record applies to these training assets, not to new
designs an adopting company creates. It grants no rights to third-party library content.

## Adoption rule

When replacing a training asset with one copied or derived from an external source,
record that source's applicable license, attribution and distribution constraints.
Retain its required notices and replace this record with the reviewed terms. Update
the matching catalog hash in the same reviewed change. The 0BSD permission for the
original fixture is separate from engineering or manufacturing approval.
