# Provenance — status-LED training library

## Origin

The symbols and footprints in this directory were authored as neutral synthetic
training material for this generic repository template. They are not copied from a
manufacturer library, a distributor download, a board vendor, or a proprietary
engineering project.

## Scope and limits

The assets exist only to exercise controlled shared-library paths, part identity
fields, native KiCad checks and review tooling. They do not assert a physical part
selection, IPC conformance, land-pattern suitability, connector mating, electrical
rating, 3D fit, supplier availability or manufacturing approval.

## Adoption rule

Before a library asset is used for an engineering or production project, replace
this record with the source identity, upstream revision or retrieval date, permitted
use terms, review owner and evidence appropriate to that asset. Update the matching
catalog hash in the same reviewed change.
